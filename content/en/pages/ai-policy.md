## 1. General Position

Zhovten Games openly uses modern computational tools, including generative AI systems, as part of lawful creative and production workflows.

We regard such tools as instruments comparable to engines, editors, cameras, samplers, synthesizers, or code assistants.

## 2. Technologies We May Use

Our workflows may include tools such as:

- OpenAI systems
- Suno systems
- automation tools
- editing tools
- internal pipelines
- future equivalent technologies

Tooling may change over time.

## 3. Use Cases

AI-assisted systems may be used for:

- ideation
- drafting
- editing
- prototyping
- code assistance
- research support
- localization support
- audio experimentation
- production acceleration

## 4. Human Responsibility

Final direction, curation, selection, integration, and publication decisions remain human-controlled.

Outputs may be heavily modified, recombined, rewritten, or used only as intermediate material.

## 5. Intellectual Property Position

Use of third-party AI tools does not transfer ownership of our original:

- brands
- lore
- game systems
- site architecture
- narrative settings
- proprietary creative direction

Except where third-party published terms explicitly require otherwise.

## 6. Public Position on AI Debate

We respect differing opinions regarding AI technologies.

However, lawful tool usage remains our operational decision.

We may continue using such tools now and in the future.

Industrial history did not pause for anti-electricity committees, and it is unlikely to begin now.
